EpiModelConcepts

Interactive epidemic modeling concepts for teaching and research. Explore growth curve models, statistical count models, and compartmental models through simulation — adjust parameters, run instantly, and understand every result through plain-language interpretations. No R, no installation, runs entirely in the browser.

📐
Core concepts
Differential equations, R₀ and Rₜ, serial interval, generation time, doubling time, herd immunity threshold, model taxonomy, and a full glossary. Includes an interactive SIR explorer.

Phenomenological models
Interactive simulator for growth curve models — Exponential, GGM, Logistic, Richards, Gompertz, and GRM. Adjust parameter sliders, explore example presets, and see live incidence and cumulative curves update instantly. Auto-generated plain-language interpretation for every simulation.

Statistical models
Interactive simulator for statistical epidemic count models — Poisson GLM, Negative Binomial GLM, Log-linear, ARIMA, and GAM. Generate synthetic epidemic data, visualise observed counts against the true mean, and explore how each model's assumptions shape what it sees.
Poisson GLM Negative Binomial ARIMA GAM

SIR / SEIR Explorer
Interactive compartmental model simulator. Choose from SI, SIR, SEIR, SEIR with vital dynamics, or SEIR with waning immunity. Adjust parameters and initial conditions, run RK4 simulations, and get auto-generated plain-language interpretations of key quantities — R₀, peak timing, final size, endemic equilibrium, and recurrent waves.
