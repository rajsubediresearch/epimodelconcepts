EpiModelConcepts

Interactive epidemic modeling concepts for teaching and research. Fit real outbreak data to growth models, explore key quantities, and understand every result through three-layer definitions — concept, interpretation, and implication. No R, no installation, runs entirely in the browser.

📐
Core concepts
Differential equations, R₀ and Rₜ, serial interval, generation time, doubling time, herd immunity threshold, model taxonomy, and a full glossary. Includes an interactive SIR explorer.

Phenomenological models
Fit exponential, logistic, Richards, Gompertz, and sub-epidemic wave models to real outbreak data. Compare all models simultaneously. Full error metrics (RMSE, MAE, MAPE, R², AIC, BIC) with three-layer interpretations. Paste your own data or use built-in datasets.
Logistic Richards Gompertz Sub-epidemic Model comparison

🧮
Statistical models
Log-linear, Poisson, and Negative Binomial regression fitted to count data. Automatic dispersion check (VMR) with model recommendations. Growth rate, doubling time, and rate ratio interpretation. Residual diagnostics and model comparison.
